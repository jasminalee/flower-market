# Product Traceability Module

## Module Overview

The Product Traceability module provides transparency into the origin and quality of flower products sold on the platform. Merchants can create and update traceability records for their products, documenting details such as growing origin, planting conditions, quality certifications, and supply chain data. Customers and merchants can view this information on the product page to build trust and support informed purchasing decisions.
